10 KM*

Between lakeshore and forest, flat, one aid station at the halfway point and the finish at Village LIGHT ON. Perfect for a first bib or an easy comeback.

Distance10 km
Start11:00am
Price€19
Register for the 10 KM*

Who's it for?

A first 10K, regular runners, or anyone up for running together as a family. Marked course, flat, no technical difficulty.

Aid stations

One liquid aid station at km 5, and the full aid station at the finish. Semi-self-sufficient race: carry something to drink between aid stations.

No cut-off time

The 10 KM* is a marked course with no cut-off time: run at your own pace.

21.1 KM**

The full loop of the Lac de Madine: three aid stations on course, an early start and the lake as your guide the whole way.

Distance21.1 km
Start9:00am
Price€24
Register for the Semi-Marathon**

Who's it for?

For runners from Junior category up who want to test themselves on the half-marathon distance, on a flat, marked course.

Aid stations

Liquid at km 6, liquid and food at km 12 and 15, plus the full aid station at the finish. Semi-self-sufficient race: carry something to drink between aid stations.

No cut-off time

The Semi-Marathon** is a marked course with no cut-off time: run at your own pace.
